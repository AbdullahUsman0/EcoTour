# EcoTour Pakistan Knowledge Base

## Skardu
Skardu is a gateway to Baltistan and high-altitude destinations. Key attractions include Shangrila, Upper Kachura Lake, Satpara Lake, Deosai National Park, and nearby trekking routes. Peak season is usually late spring to early autumn. Weather can change quickly, and roads can be affected by landslides.

## Hunza
Hunza Valley offers Karimabad, Baltit Fort, Altit Fort, Attabad Lake, Passu Cones, and scenic mountain routes. Travelers should start early for long day trips and keep buffer time for road conditions.

## Swat and Kalam
Swat is popular for Malam Jabba, Mingora, and green valleys. Kalam, Mahodand Lake, and Ushu Forest are often best visited with a local guide or verified transport in rainy weather.

## Islamabad to Northern Areas Route Notes
For routes from Islamabad to Gilgit-Baltistan, trips are often sensitive to weather and road conditions. Reliable planning includes one backup day, fuel budget flexibility, and emergency contacts.

## Eco-Friendly Travel Guidelines
- Prefer reusable bottles and avoid plastic waste.
- Use local guides and community-run accommodations.
- Respect protected ecosystems and do not leave litter in mountain zones.

## Safety & Crisis
Rescue 1122 is the primary emergency service in Pakistan. In mountainous regions, travelers should share live location, keep emergency cash, and check weather alerts before moving.

## Budget Planning Heuristics
Trip budgets depend on distance, fuel dynamics, season pressure, lodging tier, and group size. Shoulder season usually lowers accommodation costs compared to peak months.
